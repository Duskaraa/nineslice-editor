Help

A nineslice is a texture cut into 9 parts by 4 guides. The corners keep their size, the edges stretch along one side and the center stretches both ways. Bedrock stretches, it does not tile.

The steps

  1. Texture: load a png by dropping it anywhere, pasting it or choosing a file, or try the example. A json restores the guides.
  2. Slice: move the guides or type the insets, in texture pixels, in the order left, top, right, bottom. The preview shows any control size.
  3. Export: copy or download the json. It has the same name as the png and goes in the same folder.
  • Download pack: a .mcpack with the png, the json and a manifest. Open the file and Minecraft imports it.
  • Pack + source (.zip): the .mcpack plus the plain pack folder in source/, to keep editing. Unzip it, then open the .mcpack to import.

Mouse and keys

  • Drag a guide to move it.
  • Drag empty space, or hold Space, to move the view.
  • Wheel or pinch to zoom.
  • Arrows nudge the selected guide, Shift = 5.
  • S hides the slices.

Problems

Click a problem to jump to its field. Many have a fix button. The usual mistakes:

  • base_size is not the png size.
  • Left + right (or top + bottom) are wider than the texture, so the corners overlap.
  • All insets are 0, so it is a plain image.
  • The json has a different name or folder than the png.

Good to know

  • base_size must be the png size.
  • A control's own nineslice_size wins over the json.
  • Your work is saved in this browser. Start over is in About.
  • Nothing is uploaded.
